Style Guide

This style guide is a compilation of tips and tricks I have experimented with while designing websites.

Autumn

The Autumn palette blends the warmth of Butternut with the stability of Coffee, creating a solid foundation for earthy, inviting designs. Celosia adds an energetic accent that draws attention without overpowering, while Sage provides a versatile neutral that balances the set. This combination works especially well for projects that aim to feel approachable, grounded, and subtly vibrant.

Colors

When choosing colors for any design, it is important to first consider what you, as a designer, wish to communicate to your audience. Do you want something commanding or expressive? Or do you prefer refined, calm, and elegant?

The colors you choose for your design will communicate your intent to your audience, so it is important to choose wisely!
